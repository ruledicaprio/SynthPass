- **Under `SYNTHPASS_OCR_STOP=clean`, Tier 1 no longer always parses the full retry-loop
  concatenation.** The retry loop appends every pass's candidate lines to the page text, and
  `mrz`'s unanimity gate could refuse the loop's own decision when disagreeing lines from other
  passes were still in it. Tier 1 now reads the confirming pass's lines on a
  `variant_valid_confirmed` stop (two passes agreed field for field), and, when the loop ends on a
  damaged-capture reading it held unconfirmed, the text as it stood when that reading was first
  held: what `first-valid` would have read. Every other stop reads the full concatenation, and
  Tier 2 and the saved `.md` file still get every line (`OcrPage::tier1_text()`). **Default
  (`first-valid`) runs are unchanged**: nothing is held and nothing is confirmed under the default
  stop mode, so `tier1_text()` is always the full text there (#508,
  [ADR-0025](knowledge/decisions/ADR-0025-tier1-reads-one-pass-only-when-two-agree.md)).
