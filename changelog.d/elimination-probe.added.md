- **An `elimination_probe` example measures whether glyph geometry separates MRZ classes on the public real specimens.** `cargo run -p synthpass-bench --release --example elimination_probe` measures items 1 and 2 of the plan in `knowledge/research/mrz-geometric-elimination.md`. It locates each MRZ line from ink alone, without OCR, and accepts a line only when every cell holds exactly one connected component. Per cell it measures the top edge at four fractions of the cell's ink and the share of ink in the left quarter of the glyph. It then reports, against the reviewed fixtures' classes, how far digits stand above letters within each line, down to which resolution that holds when the band is resampled, and how well the left quarter separates `K` from the filler `<`. Output is counts and geometry by class, never a character string: tables on stdout and a local `cells.jsonl`, plus optional overlays for checking the segmentation by eye. It refuses an in-tree `--out` that git does not ignore. It is a measurement tool: nothing in the shipped pipeline changes, and results are labelled "Observed on public real specimens, ink-only segmentation, reviewed-fixture truth".
