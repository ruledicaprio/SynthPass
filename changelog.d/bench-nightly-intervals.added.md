- **`tools/bench_nightly_intervals.py` prints the nightly `bench-data` error shares per format and per profile, each with its n and a 95% Wilson interval.** For every pool of nights it reports the Tier-1 hit rate, the share of hits wrong on at least one scored field and the share wrong on each of the 12 fields. Nights pool only when the generator fingerprint, the OCR model hashes, the OCR and MRZ arms and `ocr_env` knobs, and the retry budget and time limit all match, and only while they are consecutive (a key that returns starts a new pool), so a re-render or a new model starts a new pool; the fresh slice is pooled across a pool's nights, the fixed slice never is (the same seeds add no n) and the tool says whether every night read every seed identically. Rows the retry budget cut off are left out of every rate and counted on their own line. Markdown on stdout or `--json`, aggregates only, standard library only; it reads the rows through the advisory's helpers and changes neither the advisory nor its exit codes. The method note in `knowledge/benchmarks/README.md` says what it pools and why, and that comparing two pools is the advisory's z-test.
