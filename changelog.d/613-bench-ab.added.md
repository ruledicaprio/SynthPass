- **`bench-ab.yml`: a dispatched before/after A/B of two refs on the five synthetic formats, both arms on one GitHub runner.** It builds each ref's `synthpass-bench`, pins `SYNTHPASS_OCR_MAX_SECONDS=600` on both arms so machine speed cannot enter the diff, diffs them with `tools/bench_ab_diff.py`, and keeps the reports and full diff in a 3-day artifact while the log carries counts only. Dispatch inputs pass through `tools/bench_ab_args.py`, which accepts only an allowlist of measurement knobs, and `expect_identical` turns an A/A dispatch into a neutrality check of the workflow itself (ADR-0027).
