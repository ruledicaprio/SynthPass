- **`synthpass-gen` has a validated layout model.** A `ValidatedLayout` is the portrait and visual-zone rectangles of one format, checked at construction (no empty, overflowing, overlapping or out-of-area rectangle, and every value the generator can draw fits its box), on an engine-owned canvas, watermark and MRZ; the five built-ins go through the same checks and the new `*_with_layout` entry points take one. Visual-zone text that begins with a glyph hanging left of its pen position (the sans `J` and `V`, among others) now starts up to a pixel further right so its ink stays inside its labelled box, which changes the pixels of the MRV renders and of any such text on the other formats.
